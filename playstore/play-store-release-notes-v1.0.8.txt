Versi 1.0.8

Update ini fokus pada kestabilan bacaan dan kenyamanan penggunaan.

• Menstabilkan tampilan daftar hadits Arba'in Nawawi, termasuk tampilan dan pergerakan layar.
• Memperbaiki tampilan daftar doa agar lebih konsisten.
• Penyesuaian baca dan navigasi di beberapa layar.
• Minor perbaikan pengalaman browsing doa dan hadits.

Al-Qur'an, terjemahan, tafsir, audio, doa, hadits, jadwal sholat, kiblat, kalender Hijriyah, dan bookmark tetap tersedia seperti sebelumnya.

---

English reference notes

Versi 1.0.8 focuses on reading stability and usability.

• Stabilized the Arba'in Nawawi hadits list display and navigation.
• Improved consistency in the doa list display.
• Adjusted reading and navigation on several screens.
• Minor browsing improvements for the doa and hadits lists.

Existing features remain unchanged: Qur'an, translation, tafsir, audio, duas, hadits, prayer times, qibla, Hijri calendar, and bookmarks.
